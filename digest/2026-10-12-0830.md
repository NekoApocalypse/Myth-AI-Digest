# Daily AI Digest — 2026-10-12

## The Headline

**500B tokens later, a team of AI agents decompiled a AAA game to compilable C++ — and discovered that readable code means nothing without semantic verification.** The real story isn't decompilation; it's the hard-won lessons on agent orchestration at scale: context compaction at 42%, hourly instruction re-injection to prevent focus drift, and a byte-matching "oracle" that finally gave agents an objective PASS/FAIL signal. No oracle, no correctness. <https://momo5502.com/posts/2026-10-09-game-decompilation/>

## The Stack

**[Agents] Recursive Self-Improvement through Multi-Agent Self-Supervision (MASS)** — Sakana AI ships a framework where one model fills all three roles (solver, optimizer, evaluator), searching for optimal multi-agent workflows then distilling team intelligence back into weights. Evaluated externally by GPT-5.5 and Claude Opus 4.8, MASS improves score-per-output-token on research benchmarks with zero external supervision. The "optimizee is the optimizer" loop is now a working artifact. <https://pub.sakana.ai/mass/>

**[Figures] Karpathy: The LLM Capability Gap is a Funnel, Not a Divide** — ~6B people never touched LLMs; ~20M professionals see agents do weeks of work from a prompt; ~5,000 with frontier internal access watch swarms of thousands of agents running multi-week software mega-projects. Driven by ambition scale, jagged capability peaking in verifiable digital domains, and access tiers. The vertigo at the top and indifference at the bottom are happening simultaneously. <https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046>

**[Agents] Plimsoll — A Sandbox That Reports Its Own Walls** — Self-hosted sandbox for AI agent code with four isolation tiers (process → container → kernel → VM) stamped on every result. Capability grants let agent code call your APIs without ever holding credentials. Integrates with Vercel AI SDK, Google ADK, Agno, and CrewAI. The Plimsoll line, but for sandbox boundaries. <https://github.com/plimsollmark/plimsoll>

**[Hardware] BetterWispr — Local Dictation That Stays on Your Mac** — Apache 2.0 macOS app running Whisper, Parakeet, or Apple Speech entirely on-device. Nine built-in models, per-app writing styles, meeting transcription with local summarization via Ollama or Apple Intelligence, and vocabulary learning. No account, no cloud fallback. A clean demo that local inference for everyday tools is production-ready. <https://betterwispr.com/>

**[Breakout] Terence Tao: Math 2.0** — Tao's Caltech slides on AI-assisted mathematical discovery. The HN firestorm (304 pts, 359 comments) centers on the alignment question: if a machine writes the proof, who understands it? Tao frames it as a new epistemology — Math 2.0 isn't about replacing mathematicians, but about what "understanding" means when the collaborator is non-human. <https://teorth.github.io/tao-web/slides/math-2-0-caltech-2026.pdf>

Meanwhile, **BetterWispr** (145 points) is a polished product, not a research artifact — good engineering, but the HN enthusiasm says more about appetite for local-first AI than about the app itself.

## Vibe Check

Today's front page converges on a single theme: **agents need guardrails, not just capability**. The decompilation team learned that without an objective oracle, agents hallucinate architecture. MASS builds self-supervision into the training loop. Plimsoll stamps isolation tiers on every result. BetterWispr keeps models local by design. The community is done being impressed that agents *can* do things — they're now asking whether the thing done was *correct*, and whether the walls held. 😼
