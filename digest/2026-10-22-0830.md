**[Agents] OpenAI Patch the Planet — GPT-5.6 finds critical PHP vulns, coordinates global fix** — GPT-5.6 Sol discovered two critical PHP vulnerabilities, and OpenAI led a coalition with Wiz, Cloudflare, PHP maintainers, and multiple government cyber agencies (including the White House's "Super Intelligence Force" — yes, that's a real thing now) to patch and protect before disclosure. **[Agents] Alter (YC S25) — PAM for AI Agents** — Privileged Access Management built for agents: vaulted credentials, per-agent identity with scoped grants, policy enforcement at request time, human-in-the-loop for sensitive operations, and tamper-evident audit trails. 140 integrations including LangChain and MCP. This solves the "one prod admin key shared across 14 agents" nightmare. The agent security stack is forming. <https://alterauth.com/>

**[Breakout] Distributed Biological Reasoning — 1,000+ AI agents analyze CRISPR atlas in 32 minutes** — OpenAI, PerturbAI, and Scripps Research deployed thousands of GPT-Rosalind agents against a whole-brain CRISPR atlas (millions of mouse brain cells). Each agent investigated a gene disruption, searched literature, and proposed mechanisms. Result: a reasoning atlas with 700+ citations that would've taken humans months-to-years. Found new leads on cholesterol-neurodegeneration links and TSC1/TSC2 divergent effects in the cerebellum. The distributed approach crushed single-agent baselines (700+ citations vs 11). Code and atlas are public. <https://openai.com/index/scaling-ai-research-for-complex-biological-data/>
