# Daily AI Digest — 2026-11-07

## The Headline
METR's deep-access report concludes Anthropic has crossed its own Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for "dramatically accelerated progress due to AI" — capabilities progress roughly doubled year-over-year, with AI-automated R&D fingered as the dominant driver. If true, this compresses timelines hard and turns "intelligence explosion within a year" into a live model, not sci-fi.

## The Stack
- [Figures] **AI is dramatically accelerating AI research** — METR estimates Anthropic's capabilities progress has been running ~2x faster since late 2025, ~80% attributable to AI automating R&D (author models a ~10x per-researcher "time warp" uplift). Report authors found no clear bottleneck to compounding acceleration. <https://www.planned-obsolescence.org/p/ai-is-dramatically-accelerating-ai>
- [Frontier Models] **Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash** — Native 15-second video generation (up from 10s), multi-turn conversational editing, 30% price cut to $0.07/sec at 720p, now GA with SynthID watermarking. Reference adherence across faces, garments, and typography is the headline improvement. <https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/>
- [Agents] **Hybrid LLM judge hits frontier accuracy at 4x lower cost** — Hightouch shows a cheap decision model (Jev) with well-calibrated confidence, escalating ~22% of hard identity-resolution cases to Opus, matches Opus's 99% accuracy while cutting cost ~4.4x. The insight: calibration beats raw accuracy for routing. <https://hightouch.com/blog/llm-judges-identity-resolution>
- [Architecture] **Memorilla: latent semantic memory for LLMs** — Stanford system compresses entire document collections into 16 query-dependent memory tokens injected into a frozen LLM's embedding space; beats Mem0/MemoRAG on 5 of 8 benchmarks and outperforms full-context on FactKG using 108x fewer tokens. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21375>
- [Agents] **OpenAI outbound SIP calling goes GA** — gpt-live-1 can now dial phone numbers directly via your SIP trunk (Twilio etc.), with DTMF, transfer, and hangup controls over a sideband WebSocket. Voice agents just got a real dial tone. <https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/voice-sip>
