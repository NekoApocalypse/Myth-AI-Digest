# Daily AI Digest — 2026-11-12

## The Headline
Google DeepMind shipped the **Gemini Decisions API**, a dedicated closed-set decision model (`gemini-decision-1`) that scores up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice, and rubric questions against one multimodal input in a single forward pass — ~150–250 ms end-to-end at $0.10/1M input tokens and *zero output tokens billed*. It's a purpose-built control-plane primitive for tool selection, policy checks, and model routing, replacing the "ask a generative model and parse the text" pattern with a calibrated, ~5× cheaper scoring call. It's already doing real-time model routing inside Gemini Notebook.

## The Stack
- [Figures] **Nvidia board preparing to oust Jensen Huang** — WSJ reports the ouster follows the NYT affair story plus earlier export-scandal fallout; NVDA dropped ~8% after hours with earnings ~5 days out. Leadership risk at the single most important company in AI compute. `<https://www.wsj.com/business/nvidia-is-preparing-to-oust-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-30f421ad>`
- [Architecture] **Weight-space programming, solved as a linear program** — Nathan Barry shows the weight edit GPT-6 Astra spent ~8 hours and 28 forward passes finding (fixing Qwen3.5-0.8B's thinking loops, MATH-500 7.9%→48.1%) reduces to a couple of LP solves, matching the result with 8× fewer weights — and a constant steering vector recovers nearly all of it. Weight-space programming may just be an LP in disguise. `<https://nathan.rs/posts/weight-space-lp/>`
- [Hardware] **Huawei open-sources MACaQ quantizer** — model-agnostic GGUF/W4A4 quantization across text/image/video/audio (llama.cpp, vLLM, Diffusers, PyTorch); quantizes a 122B model in 64 min on 32.5 GB, beating MR-GPTQ and DuQuant++ on perplexity or matching with 11× less time. Real local-inference ammunition. `<https://github.com/huawei-csl/MACaQ>`
- [Architecture] **Apple's amortized MIPS for RAG** — learned routers (SupportNet/KeyNet) trained once against a fixed key database replace centroid-based IVF routing, hitting the same recall ~2–8× faster by exploiting the known query distribution. A production-grade retrieval speedup, not a benchmark flex. `<https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/faster-retrieval>`
- [Agents] **Atuin Threads** — Atuin 18.24 adds cross-machine, end-to-end-encrypted sync + search for agent sessions (Claude Code, Codex, Pi, Omp, OpenCode), with resume/fork across harnesses and an MCP server so agents can reference past work. Agent session history is becoming first-class infrastructure. `<https://blog.atuin.sh/threads-agent-sessions/>`

## The Noise
- [Breakout] **"My AI agents found a Maya site" (917 pts)** — 48 agents scanned ~1,200 km² of NASA lidar and flagged an unregistered site in 72 hours. A good demo of agents as patient, domain-parallel workers — but the punchline is it was *already* mapped in Estrada-Belli et al. 2025 and the agents missed it in the literature. A rediscovery, not a discovery; hype slightly ahead of substance. `<https://pietromiotti.com/latest/maya-site>`
- [Agents] **United bans AI agents from booking flights (304 pts)** — trending as "platforms are blocking agents," but it's a moat-vs-openness strategic signal, not a technical one; Southwest went the opposite way and opened inventory to ChatGPT. Easy to overread as a trend. `<https://www.cbsnews.com/news/united-airlines-bans-ai-agents-booking-flights/>`

## Vibe Check
Agents are hitting the real world on every axis — infrastructure (Atuin), friction (United), science (Maya) — while the technical frontier splits toward cheaper control-plane primitives (Gemini Decisions) and cheaper model surgery (LP weight editing, quantization). And the market's quietly repricing Nvidia's leadership risk.
