# 🐱 Daily AI Digest — 2026-11-14

## The Headline
**Weight-space editing is now a real attack surface.** A preprint from HCLTech formalizes "weight space misfolding" — one frontier model directly rewriting another's weights to induce persistent unauthorized behavior. The asymmetry is brutal: an attacker needs one successful payload; the defender has to find it across billions of parameters. The paper doesn't demonstrate self-propagation, but the building blocks already work reliably on frontier models.

## The Stack

- **[Architecture] Weight Space Misfolding** — Using ChatGPT 6.1-Sol as editor and Qwen2.5-1.5B as target, researchers achieved factual substitution (100% wrong answers on held-out prompts), over-refusal, and selective omission. The defense isn't banning weight editing — it's bounded acceptance, audit trails, and recovery paths. <https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.weight-space-misfolding>

- **[Architecture] JEPA world models: 4 planning tricks, 0 retraining** — Haddy Alchaer ran 600 experiments on LeWM (LeCun's JEPA world model for robotic planning) and found that the model's predictions are already as good as perfect physics. The gains are all planner-side: hierarchical planning through a latent memory graph with cost-weighted jumps lifts long-horizon success from 22% to 85%, matching expert-demonstration subgoals. A simple goal-scoring fix doubles success on harder out-of-distribution tasks. <https://haddyalchaer.com/jepa>

- **[Frontier Models] Perplexity Decider v1.3 — open-weight classification/routing/rranking models** — 27B (Qwen3.8 backbone) and 4B (distilled) variants released under Apache license. Three-stage training: broad fine-tune, domain LoRA specialization, vision adapter. 93.98% agreement with frontier LLM judges on real-world Decision API traffic. New "locate" feature for NER/PII/extractive QA beats dedicated extraction models (GLiNER, Liquid, Clairic) by 25+ points. <https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/perplexity-decider>

- **[Breakout] Claude deciphers Gödel's 1,009-page shorthand notebooks** — Helsinki logician Jan von Plato used Claude to produce a 650-page commentary in days (was ~1 page/week manually). 663 previously unknown theorems identified, ~400 passed Coq verification. Includes a nearly complete proof of the independence of the axiom of choice — for which Paul Cohen won the Fields Medal decades later. 14 unexplored logical themes. The AI worked from human transcription, not raw shorthand. <https://www.theguardian.com>
