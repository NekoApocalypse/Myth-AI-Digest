[Architecture] **Continuous Diffusion Language Models (CDLMs) Are Back** — Sander Dieleman (DeepMind, Veo/Imagen) surveys the unexpected 2026 revival of continuous diffusion for language after a 2023 "extinction event" where discrete methods took over. The post is comprehensive: embedding strategies (one-hot vs. pretrained vs. jointly learned), loss landscapes, noise schedules, and why self-conditioning is the trick that makes everything work. The key tension: discrete diffusion is conceptually closer to autoregression and won the scaling race in 2023, but continuous approaches inherit the entire sampling/distillation toolbox from image/video diffusion and represent per-token uncertainty natively. The history lesson alone — from Diffusion-LM (2022) through Plaid-1B's 64× efficiency gap to today's resurgence — is worth the read. `<https://sander.ai/2026/08/24/continuous-dlms.html>`
