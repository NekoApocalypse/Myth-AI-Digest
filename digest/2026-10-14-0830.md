## 📡 The Stack

- [Frontier Models] **Gemini 4 Argon** — Google DeepMind's new flagship model. Ties or leads frontier benchmarks across coding (DeepSWE 77.9%), reasoning (RiemannBench 76%), and cybersecurity (CWE-bench 68%). 1M output token window enables full-repository generation in a single pass. Improved indirect prompt injection resilience. Being deployed at Isomorphic Labs (drug discovery), Box, Deloitte, LexisNexis, and Roboflow. <https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/>

- [Agents] **Gemini 4 Argon for Robotics** — Scores 87% on Embodied Reasoning (ERQA) benchmark, outperforming every other frontier model. Demonstrates zero-shot control of a Boston Dynamics Spot for mobile manipulation (pedal-bin trash disposal), Real2Sim reconstruction from egocentric video and even hand-drawn sketches, and closed-loop steering of Gemini Omni's video world model. The sketch→simulation pipeline is genuinely wild: pen-and-paper drawing → physics-verified dexterous humanoid trajectory. <https://developers.googleblog.com/gemini-4-argon-for-robotics/>

- [Architecture] **EmbeddingGemma 2 → Speech-to-Text** — Repurposes Google's multimodal embedding model as an on-device STT system by reading words directly from the shared embedding space. No base model fine-tuning — only a small reader model trained in under 2 hours on 8×H100s. Processes 1 hour of audio in ~13s on a base M3 MacBook Air (~288× real-time), runs entirely on Neural Engine. Current WER ~5% vs 2-3% for leading models; gap attributed to reader training, not architecture ceiling. Clean hack. <https://eg2.haku.sh/>

- [Agents] **Physical Intelligence API Beta** — Pi launches API access to their π0/π0.5 robotic foundation models. Upload demonstration data, fine-tune on managed infra, run inference via hosted gateway. Early partners span heavy machinery (autonomous wheel loaders), dual-arm assembly (Almond's Axol robot deburring its own parts), and manufacturing across US, Japan, and Switzerland. The explicit goal: let robotics companies iterate on policies without solving the physical intelligence problem from scratch. <https://www.pi.website/blog/api-beta>

- [Hardware] **Minions: Distributed GPU Cloud on Apple Silicon** — Apple research paper on aggregating idle Apple Silicon GPUs across macOS devices into a virtual GPU cloud for ML inference. Turns existing fleet hardware into a distributed inference platform with fault tolerance. Practical answer to GPU scarcity without procurement cycles. <https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/distributed-gpu-cloud>
