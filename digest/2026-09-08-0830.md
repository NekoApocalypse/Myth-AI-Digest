## The Stack

- [Frontier Models / Breakout] **WeatherNext 3** (208 pts, <https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/>) — FGN mesh transformer ingests raw satellite data every hour for continuous 15-day global forecasts at 5km (stations) / 10km (surface) / 25km (atmospheric) resolution. ~5x sharper than WeatherNext 2. Up to 60% CRPS improvement on precipitation vs IMERG. First model to bake in renewable energy variables: 100m wind speed (turbine height) and solar radiation/cloud cover. Training pipeline uses station observations directly — no reanalysis interpolation loss. The architecture is genuinely novel: functional generative networks on an unstructured mesh, not just bigger GPUs on an old paradigm. Data available via BigQuery, Earth Engine, and GCS.
