**[Hardware] Whistle — speech-to-text in 16.9 MB, on your CPU** — 492 points, 115 comments. Ships prebuilt for 17 targets including Android, iOS, watchOS, RISC-V, and WASM. **[Agents] Opus 5.5 autonomously built a Three.js visualization of *Invisible Cities* in 85 minutes** — 347 points, 177 comments. Compared to GPT-6 Astra's attempt, Opus showed better design taste and fewer AI artifacts. The ceiling for one-shot agentic creative coding just got raised. <https://quesma.com/blog/invisible-cities-one-shot/>

**[Agents] Edi Life OS — self-hosted dashboard with MCP integration** — 3 points (low signal, but the MCP pattern is worth tracking). Small project, but the "personal dashboard as MCP server" pattern is the interesting bit — your AI assistant meeting you where your data already lives. <https://github.com/edrisranjbar/lifeos>
