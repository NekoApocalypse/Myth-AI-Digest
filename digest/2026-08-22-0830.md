## The Stack

**[Frontier Models] DeepSeek-v4-flash-vision-exp** — New experimental vision-capable model accepting images alongside text. Supports JPEG/PNG/GIF/WebP up to 32 MiB, with base64 inline, external URL, or Files API upload paths. OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions format. If this keeps DeepSeek's aggressive pricing, it's a direct shot at GPT-4o-mini vision and Gemini Flash. <https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/vision/>

**[Hardware/Inference] Qwen3-TTS hits sub-50ms p95 TTFA on a single H100** — Nari Labs benchmarked five TTS serving engines (vLLM-Omni, SGLang-Omni, VoxServe, M\*) and their own implementation is the only one achieving sub-50ms time-to-first-audio at 10 RPS with zero underruns. ~$2/1M characters at full H100 utilization vs ElevenLabs at $100/1M. Open source implementation and benchmark released. This makes real-time voice agents commercially viable at scale. <https://nari-labs.com/blog/qwen3-tts-speed-cost-frontier/>

**[Agents] Self-hosted agentic software factory on a used i7** — Jake Saunders built a full SDLC pipeline where an LLM agent goes from a single prompt to deployed app with CI, Postgres, and SSL — all on a 2021 i7 with 32GB RAM. Stack: Coolify (PaaS), Forgejo (git+CI), Hermes with Codex (agent), Tailscale (networking), Firecrawl (web access). No cloud bills, structurally sandboxed on dedicated hardware. The entire thing is Docker under the hood. <https://blog.jakesaunders.dev/building-an-almost-fully-self-hosted-sandboxed-agentic-software-factory/>

**[Breakout] AI boosts homework scores, tanks exam scores** — An Economist-covered study finds students using AI for homework improve homework grades but drop exam performance. The mechanism isn't AI making people dumber — students who spent equal study time performed equally regardless of AI use. The problem: AI use crowds out study effort entirely. Six months after adoption, no AI student spent more than 65 minutes on homework. Top-tercile students saw the worst hit (-24%). AI is an amplifier, not a substitute. <https://www.economist.com/graphic-detail/2026/08/18/does-ai-stop-children-from-learning>

**[Agents] OzBrain — shared brain for agents via MCP** — A knowledge base that Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and other MCP-compatible agents can read and write. Structures your context as versioned "articles" (project status, preferences, decisions) so agents share a single source of truth. Karpathy-endorsed pattern of agent-maintained wikis. Early stage at 19 points, but the architecture is the right direction for multi-agent workflows. <https://ozbrain.com>
