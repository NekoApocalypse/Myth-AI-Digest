# 🐱 Daily AI Digest — 2026-11-19

## The Headline

**Gemini 4 Neon lands.** Google ships a model that hits #2–3 on most leaderboards, costs 32–80% less per task than competitors at comparable tiers, and actually works for long-horizon agent workflows. It's not the biggest model on the block — it's the one that runs cheaper while staying competitive. That's the bet.

## The Stack

[Frontier Models] **Gemini 4 Neon** — Google's latest ranks #3 on Artificial Analysis (61), #2 on Vals Index (70.9%), and #1 on Terminal-Bench Science (70.5%). 1M context window, $3/$15 per M tokens, four configurable thinking levels. Strong coding (Terminal-Bench 4.1: 65.2%, FrontierCode 2.0: 46.2%), cyber (WebExploitBench: 51.26% SOTA at half cost), and enterprise work. Available today in AI Studio, Gemini app, and Antigravity. <https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/>

[Frontier Models] **Microsoft ships seven MAI models at Ignite** — MAI Code 2 at 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol quality for 40% of the price; MAI Code 2 Flash comparable to GPT-6 Luna at 65% cost. MAI Image 2.7 takes #1 on Artificial Analysis image editing at 1/5 the price. MAI Realtime 1 ($0.01/min) beats GPT-Realtime 2.1. Maia 200 chip hits production at 1.7x tokens/watt. The strategy is clear: undercut on price-performance, not raw capability. <https://microsoft.ai/news/microsoft-ignite-2026/>

[Architecture] **Energy-Based Reasoners: 385x more efficient inference** — Duke/UIUC/Harvard paper. A 1.8M-parameter model achieves 99.9% on Sudoku-Extreme using gradient descent on a learned energy landscape, with 345× less inference compute than the next-best model. Adaptive stopping via gradient norm. SOTA coverage on multi-solution benchmarks. The idea: treat reasoning as energy minimization, not autoregressive rollouts. Code and paper available. <https://energy-based-reasoners.github.io/>

[Frontier Models] **DaisyRose-27B: RL post-training that actually works** — RadixArk's first model, built on Qwen3.8-27B with RL on 1,200 curated long-horizon agent tasks (up to 1M token sessions). Doubles Terminal-Bench 4.1 pass@1 from 5.6% to 12.0%. Trained on 144 H200s in 18 days. Open weights (BF16/FP8/NVFP4) on HuggingFace. The interesting bit: 57% lower training cost from their custom Miles backend vs open-source Megatron. <https://www.radixark.com/blog/daisyrose-27b>

[Breakout] **OpenAI models designing chips, not just running on them** — GPT models achieved ~13% area reduction on Jalapeño's vector/matrix units (silicon-proven) and up to 14% PPA improvement on AMD's flagship GPU IP. The model reasons about RTL at expert microarchitect level, verified through Synopsys tools. AMD already included changes in a taped-out design. GPT-6.2 Astra leads on RISC-V optimization benchmarks. This is what "AI for hardware" looks like when it's not a slide deck. <https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models/>

[Agents] **Mastra Factory: open-source agent-powered SDLC** — Configurable software factory that takes issues from Linear/GitHub/Slack through triage, planning, implementation, and PR review using persistent coding agents. Human-in-the-loop at key gates. Cloud sandboxes, live preview links, Slack-native. Built on the Mastra TypeScript framework (Apache 2.0). <https://mastra.ai/factory>

[Agents] **Maritime (YC F26): infra for running millions of agents** — Fully managed cloud giving each AI agent its own isolated persistent VM ($1/agent/month). Linux/Windows/Android environments, sleep/wake orchestration, monitoring, per-agent spend tracking. Solves the "how do I give every customer their own persistent agent" problem that sandbox tools don't address. <https://maritime.sh>

[Agents] **KPMG: AI agents out-question human auditors** — In a trial on a FTSE 250 audit, 100+ AI agents went deeper, asked better questions, and identified issues the human team missed. Human oversight still critical — one agent misapplied regulatory requirements. KPMG expanding to 20 larger audits. More interesting as a signal than a benchmark: professional services automation is crossing from "productivity aid" to "doing the actual analytical work." <https://europeanbusinessmagazine.com/ai-agents-better-questions-kpmg/>
