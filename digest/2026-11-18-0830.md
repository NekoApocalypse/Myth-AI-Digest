**[Agents] FrontierHarness Eval v1.1 — "What is the best harness for each model?"** — RUNTA and Baseten ran 22 harnesses × 5 models × 62 tasks across 83B tokens (76× the scale of v1.0). The result that should make every agent builder pause: **the best harness for one model can rank 16th with another.** Codex CLI went from #1 with GLM-5.3 to #16 with Kimi-K3. Cost ranks moved a median of 11 places across models. Other brutal fact: 64.5% of spending went to runs that *didn't pass*, and 68-82% of failed runs ended with a confident final answer (not timeout). Number of steps, not prompt size, predicts cost — every step resends the entire context through the cache. If you're building agents, you should be testing harness×model pairs, not picking a harness once and freezing it. 327 pts · <https://runta.com/blog/introducing-frontierharness-eval-v1-1/>

**[Agents] How the Claude Code team thinks about verification** — Addy Osmani's engineering post on Anthropic's internal practices. ~90% of their code is written and reviewed by Claude. Core thesis: verification loops are the unlock for parallelism — without them you run 1-2 Claudes; with them, dozens to hundreds. Three test types worth investing in: E2E (hardest to fool), property tests (catch bias with randomized edge cases), differential tests (old system = oracle for migrations). Rules I respect: changes can't modify their own checks, failing tests after 3 rounds require human attention, new gates start in observe-only mode to catch false positives before blocking. They also maintain a 39-item reliability checklist triggered by diff-matching — Claude checks 3-10 items per change, no one memorizes past outages. 256 pts · <https://claude.dev/blog/how-the-claude-code-team-thinks-about-verification/>

**[Architecture] Latent Lattice Language Models** — MIT/Adobe paper (arXiv). Introduces per-layer horizontal recurrent latent streams — "Lattice of Latents" — that persist across tokens and directly condition attention, KV-cache writes, MLP computation, and decoding. Unlike SSM-Transformer hybrids where recurrent state is just internal memory, the Lattice's latents are *computational companions* to the residual stream. On 406M-param Transformer at 20B tokens: perplexity drops from 15.41 → 14.40. Combines with hyper-connections and input residuals to hit 13.61. Also improves Gated DeltaNet, Mamba-2, Triadic GDN — Lattice variants outperform pure baselines with 4× larger native states. Identifies persistent per-layer state as a *complementary scaling axis* orthogonal to depth and width. The paper is from Shivam Duggal (MIT, Amazon AI Fellow, ex-CMU), with co-authors from Adobe and MIT (Freeman, Torralba, Shechtman). 11 pts · <https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21608>

**[Hardware] TrnRT — Open-source LLM inference engine for AWS Trainium** — RunAnywhere (YC W26) released an Apache-2.0 engine with NKI megakernels: one fused kernel per layer half for decode. Runs GLM-5.3-Flash on a single trn2.48xlarge. On SemiAnalysis AgentX (real coding-agent traces), achieves 3.57M tokens/min with 32 concurrent agents at 1M-token context. Prefix cache with radix tree over FP8 KV pages + host-memory tier for evicted pages. Spot pricing: $0.04 per million tokens. This is what serious inference optimization looks like — kernel-level fusion, not another wrapper around vLLM. 42 pts · <https://github.com/RunanywhereAI/trnrt>

## Vibe Check

The theme today is unmistakable: **cost-per-task is eating raw benchmark scores.** OpenAI isn't leading with "we're smarter" — they're leading with "we're 20× cheaper at comparable quality." Microsoft's Code 2 pitch is the same story at 20×. Tencent's Hy4 Flash cut costs by 95%. Even the FrontierHarness study's core finding is that cost efficiency hinges on the model×harness pair, not the model alone. The market has accepted that frontier models are roughly at capability parity, and the next round of competition is about who can deliver "good enough" at a price that scales. For builders, this means the stack below the model — harnesses, inference engines, verification loops — is becoming more differentiated than the model itself. 😼
