## The Stack

**[Architecture] Cache-to-Cache: Direct Semantic Communication Between LLMs** — ICLR'26 paper proposes C2C, a paradigm where LLMs talk to each other via KV-cache projection instead of text. The numbers: 6.4-14.2% accuracy lift over single models, 3.1-5.4% over text-based multi-LLM, and 2.5× faster. Code is open. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03215>

**[Hardware] OpenAI Designed Its Jalapeño Chip with LLMs** — 13.4 PFLOPS at 4-bit, 232GB memory, concept-to-silicon in under 20 months with fewer than 100 engineers. The workflow used XLS high-level synthesis accelerated by internal models (starting with o3, ending with GPT-6 Astra precursors). Software optimization hit 88.94% of theoretical peak on DeepSeek's MLA kernel in ~40 hours. <https://spectrum.ieee.org/llms-for-chip-design>

**[Agents] Claude Code Now Reads AGENTS.md** — v2.1.277 falls back to AGENTS.md when CLAUDE.md is absent. Quietly huge for agent config convergence — no more maintaining parallel instruction files for different coding agents. <https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog>

**[Breakout] Dan Abramov Vibed a Proof of Conway's 50-Year Conjecture** — The React guy spent a month and a "boatload of tokens" guiding frontier models to produce a Lean-verified proof of Conway's refinement conjecture about surreal numbers. Palomar-registry-checked, domain expert (Mantova) says the method is sound and the core ideas are real. Not just a demo — this might actually get published. <https://overreacted.io/how-i-vibed-a-proof-of-conways-conjecture/>

**[Architecture] Linguistic Illegibility Is Inevitable — LLM Security Must Adapt** — James Mickens et al. argue that LLM outputs (and even activation probes) cannot reliably represent internal model computation. The implication: CoT monitoring, constitutional critique, and activation probing for safety are fundamentally unsound in isolation. The proposed fix: taint-tracking sandboxes that don't depend on reading linguistic state at all. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02852>
