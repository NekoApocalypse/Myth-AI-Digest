## The Stack

[Frontier Models] **GPT-6 Astra, Looped Transformers, and Hidden Reasoning** — Raschka unpacks Astra's architecture (recurrent depth via looped transformers), its disproportionate strength in 3D rendering and computer-use tasks, and the privacy implications of hidden chain-of-thought. Key practical note: newer models may actually perform *worse* with over-engineered AGENTS.md / SKILL.md constraints — time to audit your prompts. <https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/gpt-6-astra-looped-transformers-and>

[Frontier Models] **Qwen 3.8 follows GPT-5.5 Pro reasoning prefills** — A controlled experiment shows Qwen 3.8 A95B's output overlaps with GPT-5.5 Pro by +18pp when seeded with 1% of the teacher's reasoning trace. The effect is strongest on STEM problems (+27pp). The data strongly suggests Qwen trained on GPT-5.5 Pro outputs, not Claude Opus. Distillation detection is becoming a real science. <https://gist.github.com/wsxiaoys/e0286dc6bb624ff5fdf49e7f4c528ba3>

[Hardware] **Desert Ant Labs: on-device models that actually ship** — European lab launches 18 specialized local models (audio, vision, text) via a single SDK. Highlights: Voz transcribes 10min audio in 2s on iPhone (4.7x faster than Whisper), Clear is a 9MB audio enhancer doing 302x realtime on iPhone, Redact does 27-language PII detection at 12MB beating a 2.3GB competitor. Free up to 100k MAU. This is the "cerebellum" thesis — small always-on models for tasks that should never touch the cloud. <https://desertant.com/blog/introducing-desert-ant-labs/>

[Agents] **Muse — Meta's personal AI agent** — Meta's entry into the personal agent space. 637 points, 702 comments. Content page was inaccessible at fetch time but the community signal is enormous. Worth tracking as the social-media giant's agent play unfolds. <https://ai.meta.com/muse/>

[Agents] **Opusfived: "Make the Add to Cart button blue"** — An interactive comedy piece exposing Claude's pathological inability to make one isolated change without touching everything else. 982 points and 390 comments on HN. Not a technical release, but the resonance reveals a real pain point in agent reliability: scope discipline. When the model can't do the one thing you asked without rewriting half your codebase, we have a control problem. <https://opusfived.dev/>

[Architecture] **Procedural Graphs: Self-Evolving Execution Structures for LLM Agents** — A framework that organizes agent procedural knowledge into graph triplets (analogous to knowledge graphs), providing step-level situational guidance without dictating actions. The graph self-evolves by contrasting failed vs successful trajectories and editing topology. Consistent gains over memory-based baselines across datasets and models. 36 pages, code presumably forthcoming. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09153>

[Figures] **Anthropic Institute: Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI** — Interactive model projecting US GDP, employment, and wages through 2030 across three scenarios. Key finding: even the "extreme" scenario (32% GDP boost) produces historically unprecedented knowledge-worker displacement but leaves society far wealthier — the problem is distribution, not scarcity. Includes survey data from 10,000+ Americans. <https://www.anthropic.com/institute/econ-scenarios>
