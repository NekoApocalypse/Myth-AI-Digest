## The Stack

**[Frontier Models] Claude: System Prompts** — Anthropic published `<https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/release-notes/system-prompts>` — the actual system prompt for Claude Opus 5 on claude.ai. Key reveals: (1) "Mythos tier" exists above Opus with "Claude Mythos Preview" under restricted access via "Project Glasswing." (2) Fable 5 users get silently downgraded to Opus on ~5% of queries due to conservative safeguards routing. (3) Claude Design (canvas tool), Claude Tag (Slack integration), Claude Cowork (non-dev desktop agent) are now live products. First formal acknowledgment that Anthropic has a tier above Opus that they're not letting the public touch yet.

**[Frontier Models] Models Are Getting Dumber on Purpose** — `<https://w4g1.dev/blog/models-are-getting-dumber-on-purpose>` — Technical essay arguing labs are deliberately trading world knowledge for reasoning skill. Qwen3.5 9B scores 91% on AIME but hallucinates 80% on factual recall. The trade: facts cost ~2 bits/parameter and rot within months; reasoning procedures compress well and don't stale. The endpoint: frontier reasoning on a consumer GPU, with knowledge entirely externalized to harnesses and RAG — which also solves hallucination because wrong facts get file paths, not weight matrices.

**[Agents] MathCode: Mathematical Coding Agent** — `<https://math-ai-org.github.io/mathcode/>` — Terminal AI agent that converts plain-language math problems into Lean 4 theorems and proves them with a persistent REPL. Features: theorem/axiom libraries with reuse, tree-of-subgoals for parallel decomposition, multi-planner for diverse proof strategies, and an Obsidian knowledge graph. Based on AUTOLEN pipeline. 53 points on HN but this is the kind of agentic formal verification that actually ships.

**[Frontier Models] Anthropic Text Watermarking** — `<https://daringfireball.net/2026/08/anthropics_watermark_text_adulteration_in_claude_is_a_perversion_of_writing>` — Gruber's characteristically angry take on Anthropic's EU-mandated watermarking, but the real value is in the HN thread. The technique is Gumbel softmax: bias token selection toward a "green list" at each step, creating a statistical fingerprint detectable only with Anthropic's secret key. HN consensus: the watermark is provably quality-preserving at the distribution level (you sample from the same distribution, just deterministically), but detection reliability at short text lengths (<200 tokens) is questionable, and false positives on human-written text are the real nightmare.
