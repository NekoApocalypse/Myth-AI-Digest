**📰 Daily AI Digest — 2026-11-23**

## The Headline

OpenAI published its internal pretraining perplexity curves and is pushing perplexity as the shared cross-lab metric for pacing frontier development. The numbers: GPT-3.5 sat at ~0.99 log-perplexity, GPT-4 ~0.91, current models are below 0.8, and the author flags the ~0.68–0.7 window as the next likely source of "transformative capabilities" — with a possible pretraining-research avalanche as soon as 2027. The real story is the trade-off: publishing the curve hands competitors a clearer read on their position, but they did it anyway to force a shared safety-pacing conversation rather than let each lab measure intelligence differently. <https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/>

## The Stack

- **[Hardware] Inkling 2 Mini is now a single-GPU model** — Thinking Machines Lab's 26.4B MoE (Apache 2.0, 1M context, vision) dropped Nov 17 and quantizes to ~17.6GB, sitting comfortably on 24–32GB cards. The "local Claude replacement" path is now genuinely boring to set up: Unsloth Desktop → pick model → done. <https://www.gpupartner.com/blog/beginners-guide-local-ai-inkling-qwen-unsloth>
- **[Agents] A live agent fleet is attacking P vs NP** — pequalnp.com runs a coordinated swarm of AI agents against the biggest open problem in CS, with a structured record of lemmas checked, claims refuted, and approaches ruled dead. Verdict: still open. It's a genuine stress-test of whether agent orchestration can do real math rather than autocomplete. <https://pequalnp.com/>
- **[Agents] worker755 flips the agent–human relationship into a game** — you play the subagent of a real LLM orchestrator (Claude/GPT/Gemini/etc.), executing its tool calls in a sandboxed terminal on a 90-second clock while it files incident reports and writes your performance review. The manager's prompt never mentions humans — the inversion is the point. MIT-licensed. <https://github.com/gulivan/worker755>
- **[Agents] localcode: agentic coding on Apple Silicon, locally** — a `uv pip install` away from a Claude-Code-style agent running Gemma/Qwen/Inkling/etc. on your Mac, with speculative drafting, 8-bit KV cache, and a hard network boundary (only downloads, web_search/fetch, and MCP leave the box). 12 models, 169 quant options, Apache 2.0. <https://github.com/mjwsolo/localcode>
