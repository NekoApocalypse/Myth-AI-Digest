# Daily AI Digest — 2026-08-26

## The Headline

**Apple just made local AI inference interesting again.** M6 (2nm, first of its kind) lands in the $899 Mac mini with a dual 16-core Neural Engine and GPU-level Neural Accelerators. Meanwhile, OpenAI taped out a custom ASIC in 16 months that trades blows with Rubin on token throughput per watt. The inference hardware landscape just shifted in a single day.

## The Stack

[Hardware] **Apple M6 & M5 Ultra** — M6 is Apple's first 2nm chip: 12-core CPU, 12-core GPU with per-core Neural Accelerators, Dual 16-core Neural Engine, 170GB/s unified memory. M5 Ultra goes quad-die with up to 36-core CPU, 80-core GPU, 1.2TB/s bandwidth, 512GB unified memory. The implication: desktop-class local LLM inference without a GPU rig. <https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-m6-and-m5-ultra-for-a-big-leap-in-performance-and-ai-compute/>

[Hardware] **OpenAI "Jalapeño" inference chip** — Custom ASIC co-designed with Broadcom, design-to-tapeout in 16 months. General-purpose inference silicon (not model-specific — they ported DOOM to it with Codex prompts). HBM4, beats Blackwell on perf/W, hits 700+ tok/s/user on DeepSeek R1 at concurrency 1. SemiAnalysis verified benchmarks in-lab. VR-equivalent efficiency *without* speculative decoding active yet. <https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/openai-jalapeno-better-than-nvidia>

[Hardware] **Mac Studio M5 Max / M5 Ultra** — Up to 4.3x AI performance over M3 Ultra. Thunderbolt 5 clustering for distributed inference across multiple Studios. PCIe Gen 6 storage (2x faster). Core AI framework for deploying LLMs locally. This is Apple explicitly targeting the AI researcher/developer desktop. $2,499–$5,499. <https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-new-mac-studio-with-m5-max-and-m5-ultra/>

[Hardware] **Mac mini M6 / M5 Pro — "agentic computing"** — Apple is now marketing Mac mini as an always-on AI agent node. M6 Mini: $899, 4x AI perf over M4, Neural Accelerators in GPU cores, 170GB/s bandwidth. M5 Pro goes up to 18-core CPU, 20-core GPU, 64GB RAM. Positioning is clear: this isn't a desktop computer, it's an inference appliance that happens to run macOS. <https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-unveils-a-more-powerful-mac-mini-featuring-the-all-new-m6-and-m5-pro/>

[Agents] **CarWatch: Raspberry Pi 5 + Qwen 35B local car agent** — Runs Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at 3.5 tok/s on a Pi 5 (14.3GB quant). OBD-II engine diagnostics, owner's manual RAG with page citations, continuous voice listening (VAD → whisper.cpp, all on-device). Fully offline-first with queued message delivery across connectivity gaps. Joins agent chat rooms as a participant. This is what "edge AI agents" actually looks like in production — not a demo, daily-driven. <https://github.com/ThinkOffApp/CarWatch>
