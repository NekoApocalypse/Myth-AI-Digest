## The Stack

- **[Frontier Models] GLM-5.3 Open-Weight** (571 pts) — Post-training-only upgrade to GLM-5.2 that hits open-source SOTA on Terminal Bench 3.0 (28.3→4.6 over its predecessor), DeepSWE (66.9), and Agents' Last Exam (28.5). The real story is emergent cyber capability: ExploitGym went from 29→105 (2h) and 39→130 (6h). ExploitBench more than doubled (24.4→54.4). Apache 2.0, runs on vLLM, SGLang, Transformers, Unsloth. <https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.3>

- **[Agents] Autonomous Mathematical Discovery with Multi-Agent Systems** (75 pts) — Dualverse AI's "The Station": agents from different model families conduct autonomous math research without a central coordinator. Across 12 AlphaEvolve construction problems, agents produced novel theorems and constructions on 5 problems — new infinite-family finite-field Kakeya sets, new exact 604-point kissing configurations in dimension 11, improved Erdős minimum-overlap bound, and novel infinite families for Book Ramsey numbers. Crucially, the agents produced not just numerical results but theorems and analyses explaining *how* the constructions work. Full raw agent dialogues, proofs, and verification code released. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23691>

- **[Figures] Judge Rules Anthropic Blacklisting Illegal** (507 pts) — Federal judge ruled the Trump administration's blacklisting of Anthropic was illegal, finding First Amendment retaliation rather than legitimate national security concern. The government's entire rationale was a 4-page memo that post-dated two of the three challenged actions, and it conceded Anthropic's technology "is itself no riskier…than any other 'black box' AI model." Not just an Anthropic story — this sets precedent for how the government can (or can't) target AI labs for their speech. <https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/27/technology/anthropic-government-blacklisting-ruling.html>

- **[Breakout] AI-Driven Copyright Takedowns Nuke Open-Source Game Engine** (429 pts) — Microsoft, via Tracer.AI's automated brand-protection agents, filed a DMCA notice against Luanti (open-source voxel game engine) on Google Play, claiming Minecraft copyright infringement. Luanti ships zero proprietary game assets — it's an engine with no bundled games. This exact same Tracer.AI → Microsoft → DMCA pipeline hit Luanti in 2023 (46 days to restore) and indie game Allumeria earlier this year. AI-powered automated enforcement with no human verification, hitting the same false positive twice. The engineers are unionizing against the robots and losing. <https://blog.luanti.org/2026/08/27/luanti-dmca-tracer-ai/>
