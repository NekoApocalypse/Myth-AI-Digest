## The Stack

**[Frontier Models] DeepSeek V3 in 190 Lines of Pure JAX** — A pedagogical 36M-param implementation of MLA, DeepSeekMoE, auxiliary-loss-free balancing, and multi-token prediction. Trained from scratch on a MacBook Air M5 in ~2.6h. Every expert, every latent, every bias update is a function you can read top-to-bottom. Code ships, weights ship, training logs ship. The opposite of a black-box API. <https://github.com/Vaibhaav-Tiwari/deepseek-v3-jax>

**[Agents] Antigravity's /teamwork: Multi-Agent Research at Scale** — Google Research's orchestration system partitions context by construction: specialized agents get only the files and tools they need, coordinators hand off to fresh successors, and dedicated critics verify from clean contexts. The system proved the 2013 Courtade–Kumar conjecture (50M lines of Lean 4), resolved open problems in adaptive data analysis, quantum computing, Boolean matrix multiplication, and kernel optimization. End-to-end algorithms from open problem statements, with formal verification. <https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/>

**[Hardware] Generatie AI Uses More Energy on Non-Engish Text** — University of Michigan research finding that AI inference consumes significantly more energy when processing non-English languages. Source paywalled (403), so details are thin — but the directional implication is real: current tokenization and inference pipelines are optimized for English, and the global cost asymmetry is worth tracking. <https://news.umich.edu/generatie-ai-uses-more-energy-on-non-engish-text/>

**[Breakout] Lovable Apps Expose Passport Data — Vibe-Coding Meets Production** — Security researchers scanned 159,041 AI-built Lovable apps and found 30% of Supabase-backed projects exposed email addresses without authentication. The haul: 80K+ profiles with full PII, 200K+ chat messages, passport numbers, internal emails, contracts, invoices. The vulnerability is not a single app — it's the templated database patterns that make mass-exploitation trivially automatable. When every app shares the same AI-generated architecture, one exploit scales to thousands. <https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/>
