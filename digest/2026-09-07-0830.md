## The Stack

**[Figures] Research acceleration: The view inside OpenAI** — OpenAI's unusually detailed transparency report on RSI progress. The median researcher now integrates coding agents daily, and total agent runtime has crossed above total human labor. Task complexity is shifting up: agents are handling longer-horizon, higher-level work under Epoch AI's R&D taxonomy (Decide/Design/Build/Run/Analyze/Communicate). After the Hugging Face incident, they paused RL training on deploy-targeted models while hardening environments — a real action, not just a blog post. The self-imposed commitment to publicly track RSI progress is worth watching. <https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-view-inside-openai/>

**[Figures] An Alien Mind** — Senior OpenAI research lead (likely Szymon Sidor) lays out the alignment challenge with unusual candor. Core framing: AI is grown, not designed — a product of massive compute optimization whose internal mechanisms we study like neuroscience. Distinguishes goal alignment (does the AI try to accomplish the task?) from value alignment (does it hold and generalize human principles when unsupervised?). GPT‑6 Astra is name-dropped as the first model benefiting from significant alignment advances. The author calls for "extreme caution" and broader interventions beyond technical solutions — language that's notably sharper than OpenAI's typical public comms. <https://openai.com/index/an-alien-mind/>

**[Architecture] Harnessing the Universal Geometry of Embeddings** — arXiv paper demonstrates unsupervised translation of text embeddings between arbitrary vector spaces, zero paired data needed. High cosine similarity across different architectures, sizes, and training datasets — lending weight to the Platonic Representation Hypothesis. The security side is the punchline: an attacker with only your stored embedding vectors can reconstruct enough to classify and infer attributes about your data. Vector DB vendors, take note. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12540>
